# World Model — Slide-Door Puzzle

## Objective & Outcome
A 6-level slide puzzle played entirely through the CLI (`game.py`). Each level is a grid of tiles; the player moves a creature that **slides until it hits an obstacle**. The goal of every level is to **stop the creature on the green plant tile**. Budget is 1000 actions; every `do` and `reset` costs 1 action, `look` is free.

**Best outcome: all 6 levels completed (`state=WIN`) in 320 of 1000 actions.** Cumulative actions at each clear: L1=10, L2=15, L3=31, L4=144, L5=288, L6=320.

## Objects & State
`look` prints `state=PLAYING|WIN levels=n/6 actions_used=N/1000`, the grid as numbers (same picture = same number), and saves `frame.png`. Grid size varies per level (7×7 up to 13×15; the last is irregular).

- **9** — the creature (the player).
- **5** — green plant = level goal; passable, and the level completes the moment the creature *stops* on it.
- **6** — water, the common passable ground.
- **21** — orange box, a pushable object.
- **22–26** — the five states of a blue door; all blue doors on a level are one shared rotor.
- **27** — gold door: the permanent state a door takes after eating a box; passable.
- Other IDs (frame 0–4, 12–14; dark blocks 7, 8, 10, 11, 19, 20, 31, 34) are impassable walls/frame.

## Controls
- `ACTION1 / 2 / 3 / 4` = slide **up / down / left / right** (confirmed by probing).
- `ACTION7` = **undo**: exactly reverses the immediately previous move (opposite direction, same distance; a no-op if that move slid 0 tiles). Verified on L1–L3 with no boxes involved.

## Movement
The creature slides in the chosen direction until the next tile is an obstacle, stopping on the last free tile. If it is blocked immediately, it does not move (reports "nothing changed").

## Interactions
**Box push.** If the creature's slide runs into a box, the box slides the same way until it hits a wall *or another box* (chain pushes work), and the creature stops **one tile behind the box's final position**. A box never stops mid-path.

**Blue door = one 5-state rotor.** All blue doors share a single rotor. **Rotor rule (confirmed on L5): every creature slide advances the rotor by `(tiles slid + 1) mod 5`** — a slide of `k` tiles advances `k+1`, and a blocked 0-tile move advances 1. So the door state is a function of *cumulative slide distance*, and the length of every move must be accounted for when planning.
- Blue doors **block the creature**; which direction is blocked depends on the rotor state (5 states = 5 orientations). Observed on L4: a leftward approach *from the right* is blocked in all of 22–26, while vertical travel through the door is passable, and a creature that is *on* the door tile can slide left and exit.
- **Box absorption:** a box pushed into a blue door is absorbed (disappears) and the door becomes **gold (27)**. Gold doors are passable and absorb nothing — a box passes straight through and keeps sliding.

## Goal & Level Transitions
Stopping on the plant completes the level. The game prints `LEVEL COMPLETED (n/6)` and immediately shows the next level's grid; after level 6 it reports `state=WIN`.

## Undo / History
`ACTION7` undoes only the single most recent move (exact reverse) — no deeper history. `reset` restarts the current level and costs 1 action.

## Critical Discovery
The door rotor + box absorption is the heart of the puzzle: blue doors are one distance-driven state machine that permanently open (gold) by eating boxes. **L4 is solvable only by stacking two boxes** (step by step): (1) push box B3 up through the now-gold door so it parks at the top of the column; (2) push a second box B2 up the same column so it stops directly beneath B3; (3) the creature then rides that push-landing **exactly onto the door tile** — and from there it slides left and exits into the plant pocket, which is sealed on every other side.

A second decisive insight: because the rotor advances by *distance + 1*, you must track exact slide **lengths**, not just directions, to land on the plant at the right door state.

## Remaining Uncertainty
- Exact mapping of each door state (22–26) to its blocked direction; only the leftward case (L4) and vertical passability were tested across states.
- Whether the rotor advances on *every* move or only moves that touch a door (the L5 rule was the main confirmation).
- Whether `ACTION7` (undo) spends a budget action, and how undo interacts with box pushes (both untested).
- Whether a blocked 0-tile slide still spends an action (assumed yes).
- On L5 a dark block (tile 8) moved on its own once and was not restored by `reset` — a self-moving hazard whose trigger is unknown; it behaved as a static wall in other levels.
