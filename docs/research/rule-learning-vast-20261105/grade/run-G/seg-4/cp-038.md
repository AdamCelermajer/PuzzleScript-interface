# World Model — Slide-Puzzle Game (10×11 grid)

## Objective & Best Outcome
Clear all 6 levels within a shared budget of 800 actions. **Result: 3/6 levels completed; run ended at L4 with budget exhausted.** L1–L3 were won by reaching grass; L4's win condition was never triggered despite exhaustive testing of every reachable stop position, door, machine event, and rewind sequence.

## Objects & State
- **Grid**: 10 rows × 11 columns. Tile types seen: `0`/`1` walls (static, impassable), `3` doors, `6` floor, `5` grass, `21` crates (pushable), `19`/`20` boxes (static, solid), `7` one special door tile, `10`–`14` decorative boundary tiles, `9` player, `22`–`27` the machine (display value).
- **Player**: single token; one tile at a time.
- **Machine** (L4, mid-grid): shows a number that random-walks 22–26 while "pre-charge"; becomes fixed `27` after being fed (see below).
- **Global action counter**: increments on every action (including A7 and RESET); never reset by RESET; the 800 cap ends the run.
- **Phase**: an implicit per-action state that gates doors and anomalies; it drifts unpredictably each action (the display number correlates loosely but does not encode it).

## Controls
- `ACTION1/2/3/4` = up/down/left/right.
- `ACTION7` = time rewind (undo).
- `RESET` = restart the current level fresh.

## Movement & Interactions (observed)
1. **Slide**: pressing a direction moves the player along straight floor tiles until blocked by a wall, box, boundary, a closed door, or a crate/machine; the player stops on the last free tile.
2. **Crate push**: if a crate is the first obstacle, it is pushed as far as it can go in that line and the player stops on the tile behind the crate's final position. Crates cannot push crates, and a push whose target path is fully blocked results in **no movement at all** for either.
3. **Machine feed** (decisive interaction, step by step):
   - Push a crate up into a pre-charge machine (display 22–26) → the crate is **consumed**, the display jumps to `27` and stays there, and the player's slide *continues through the machine tile* to the far side (e.g., from below, player ends at the top row).
   - A charged (27) machine is **passable** by the player.
   - A crate pushed into an already-charged machine is usually consumed (2 of 3 observed); once (action 420) it passed through to the top row while the player stopped one row below — a one-off, not reproduced in 13 further attempts.
4. **Doors**: tiles that alternately block and pass. Observed open rates over repeated tests: one door ~25–30% (led only to already-known stops), one ~13%, the rest 0/30–0/50. Which doors open seems phase-locked; different action sequences sample different phases.
5. **Anomalies** (rare, ~5–15% on certain slides): the slide ends early or teleports (e.g., a column slide stopping mid-column, or jumping to a distant tile). No anomaly produced a win.

## Goal & Level Transitions
- L1/L2: win by **stopping on grass**. L3: win by **passing through grass** while pushing a crate. Transitioning advances the level and keeps the global budget.
- L4: win condition **unknown**. Grass exists but is provably unreachable (its row is sealed by a solid box, four never-opened wall/door tiles, and a sealed tile cluster). Every reachable stop position, door-open stop, machine event, and rewind combination was tested without a win.

## Undo & History Effects
- **ACTION7 rewinds exactly one action's full state** (player, crates, machine display/charge, and phase). It costs one budget action. Rewinding immediately after a feed undoes the feed (display reverts, crate restored); rewinding one action later restores the crate while keeping the charge. Repeated A7 walks back through history; from a restored state you may play a *different* action than the original (branching replay).
- **RESET** returns the level to its initial state (fresh crates, pre-charge machine, start position) but the global action counter keeps counting.

## Critical Discovery
The A7/RESET combination allows exact state restoration plus branching: feed the machine, perform a consuming push, then rewind to recover the "charged machine + crate present" state — a state unreachable by forward play. This was the only way to reach the player row-2 position and the crate-at-top-row configuration, both of which were tested as potential win states (no win).

## Remaining Uncertainty
- The L4 win condition. Leading untested candidates: stopping on tiles behind doors that never opened in this run's phase windows (`(8,1)`, `(6,3)`, corridor `(8,6)`), or a rare phase-locked event (e.g., a specific door open state combined with a position never co-observed, or a specific machine-pass-through occurrence).
- Whether door phases are periodic in the global counter (suggested by early-run corridor openings) or otherwise structured; the one machine pass-through may have been phase-locked to action 420.
- Behavior of the `7`-type tile, whether `1`-type walls can ever open (0/25+ tests), and what, if anything, happens at exactly 800 actions besides termination.
