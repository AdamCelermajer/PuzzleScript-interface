## What rests on n=1

Everything comparative: Max 4/4 vs Flash 1/4 vs 27B 2/4; Max ran real checks and the others did not; Flash's research found the movement rule; Max's model error being harmless; the baseline+ goal misreadings. Each is one recorded run with a model-written strategy, so none is a rate. Max's 4/4 is at least internally repeated (self-check 2 and the final evaluation are the same deterministic 265 actions), but that is one solver, not a second campaign.

The riskiest claim left is the **Flash story**: "Flash can find the rule in research but cannot deliver a solver". Its 1/4 came from one solver with a visible loop bug, its own online learner had the rule by step 80, and its NanoGPT direct-play run reached 2/4. A second Flash run could easily land anywhere from 1/4 to 4/4.

## The one extra run that would most help

One repeat **Flash AVO campaign on Modality** (same model, Alibaba routing, high effort, same budgets) on the current harness (OpenCode or bare, since the Codex route is gone). If it again reaches 2–3 research levels and delivers a weak solver, the consolidation story stands with n=2; if it delivers 3–4/4, the card must say Flash can solve the game. It is the cheapest run (roughly a tenth of a Max run) and targets the claim with the widest plausible outcome range. The harness change is a confound, so present it as "Flash on the current harness" rather than a replication.
