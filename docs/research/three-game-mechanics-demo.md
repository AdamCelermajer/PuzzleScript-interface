# Three-game mechanics comparison

This comparison uses the PuzzleScript source as ground truth and saved AVO artifacts as the agent's evidence. The old Modality and Knightoban `world_model.md` files were **reconstructed after their runs**. Their contemporaneous `STRATEGY.md`, archived research notes, solver, and recorded transitions support the findings below; the reconstructed prose is a useful summary, not a verbatim run output. IceCrates has both contemporaneous `TRAIN_FINDINGS.md` and a final in-run world model.

| Game | Ground-truth rule in `script.txt` | What the saved AVO work inferred | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Modality | Player cannot cross directly between white and black; grey bridges them. A crate is pushed when player and crate occupy compatible terrain. Win: all crates on targets. | Controlled same-checkpoint tests found a successful push from white to dark, then a blocked second push because the player could not enter the crate's dark cell. The final terrain-aware BFS uses `(player, crate)` plus terrain and reached 4/4. | **Core mechanic recovered.** Its final solver and research record support the push asymmetry and target rule. Its model leaves untested object/terrain combinations open. |
| Knightoban | Ordinary crates have a direct push rule. Knight crates contact a marker/selection mode, move to a legal knight landing, use perpendicular inputs to select, and ACTION5 to commit. Win: every target has a crate. | Saved probes separated preview from no-op, discovered two knight landings, spatial selection, ACTION5 commitment, hidden targets, and joint multi-piece planning. The final fresh solver reached 6/6. | **Main observed mechanic recovered.** The post-run world model omits the separate ordinary-crate source rule; the saved evidence does not establish every unobserved normal-crate case or exact single-option preview behavior. |
| IceCrates | A direction input repeatedly slides the player. Adjacent crates push as a train; crates entering water disappear and fill it. Win: a stopped player on the goal. | The inner agent falsified win-on-crossing, distinguished boxes from walls, reproduced water filling, and found the two-box train that the first solver lacked. `TRAIN_FINDINGS.md` documents the controlled comparison; the final independent solver won 6/6 in 138 actions. | **Core mechanic recovered**, including the decisive train collision and goal-at-rest condition. Animated water phase equivalence is supported only for tested continuations. |

## What “learned manually” means here

The AVO controller stages `avo/`, `client/`, and `studies/` into its isolated container, not `puzzlescript_interface/games/`. The inner prompt says to use only interface observations; environment actions and fresh solver evaluations are saved separately. The source rules above were read for this **post-run audit**, not supplied to the evaluated agents. This supports an observation-derived interpretation of the successful solvers, but a successful score alone does not prove that every verbal rule is exact. The saved notes include false hypotheses and their corrections, which provide stronger evidence of actual discovery.
