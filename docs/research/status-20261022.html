Qwen 3.8 27B: where we stand

Adam Celermajer · 22 October 2026

The 27B can learn these games. On its first attempt, with no help, it solved Knightoban and IceCrates and got 3 of 4 levels in Modality.

It needed harness work that bigger models didn't. The biggest change was letting it drive the game itself.

It still can't find a rule that leaves no visible trace: Modality's floor colours.

How we got here

  • Bigger models solved these games in the old setup: DeepSeek Flash solved all three, Qwen Max solved Modality.
  • For the 27B we had to fix:
    • runs ending on one bad reply;
    • a 30-image limit (DeepInfra);
    • NanoGPT dropping Codex's tools;
    • OpenCode never sending the picture;
    • context overflow (now compacted at about 198k tokens);
    • AVO filling its context with every past board;
    • a 200-move budget, when Modality needs at least 263.
  • Biggest change, letting the agent drive the game (20 Oct): level 1 at move 16 instead of 54, and level 2 at move 62 instead of never.
