## Decided
- OpenCode sessions declare image input from the provider's model list and record `image_input` in `session.json`; opencode results before this date are "no image" runs and are not comparable with later ones.
- Treat harness files as fixed inputs restored after every turn; keep notes off and the starting harness empty. Permit the full upstream component surface, including subagents.
- Resume reuses completed rollout IDs and upstream proposal caches; interrupted players restart individually. Wall limits apply per session, with upstream's 3600s default for improvers when unset.
- Limit pytest discovery to maintained `tests/` and `frameworks/`; unrestricted discovery imported generated solver scripts from `output/`.
