# Proposal revision, 21 October 2026 (evening draft, not pushed to Overleaf, not committed)

For Adam to review before anything is uploaded. `main.tex` and `refs.bib` changed; nothing else in

## Why

The 21 October audit and the self-hosted experiment changed the premise
(`docs/research/premise-review-20261021.md`). The old proposal asked *why* Qwen 3.8-27B fails and
whether the cause is the harness, its size or its pretraining (H1–H4 on the revision loop, goal and
control bottlenecks, externalised planning, localisable gaps). Today the same model, driving the game
itself with its own scripts and a 1000-move budget, solved 2 of 3 games near-optimally and reached 3/4
on the third; its one surviving failure is a hidden rule. The proposal now asks under which conditions
it learns, what blocks it on a hidden rule, and whether same-model coaching transfers learning rather
than answers.
