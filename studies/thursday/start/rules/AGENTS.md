# Notes about this game

What the actions do: ACTION1 = up, ACTION2 = down, ACTION3 = left, ACTION4 = right, ACTION7 = undo your last move.

Floors are white, dark or grey (checkered).
- You cannot step directly from white onto dark or from dark onto white. Grey can be entered from either and left onto either.
- Walking into the orange square frame (the box) pushes it one cell, if that cell is inside the board. The box can land on any floor.
- You can push the box only from a matching floor: standing on white or grey you can push a box that stands on white or grey; standing on dark you can push a box that stands on dark. Any other push does nothing.
